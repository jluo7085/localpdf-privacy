LocalPDF Suite Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026 · Publisher: Revolware

LocalPDF Suite processes PDF files on your device. The extension does not upload your documents, file names, page contents or generated results to Revolware or another server.

Files you choose

When you choose or drop files, the extension reads them into the current workspace to inspect page counts and perform the operation you request. Documents and working results remain in that tab until you clear the list or close the tab. The extension does not save document contents in browser storage.

Downloads are saved by your browser to the location you choose or have configured. Clearing the workspace, closing the tab or uninstalling the extension does not delete downloaded files or your original documents.

Local preferences

The extension stores one theme preference, localpdf_theme, in localStorage on your device. It is not sent to a server and does not contain document data. Use the button below from the installed extension's privacy page to remove this preference, or remove the extension and clear its site data using your browser.

The public web copy of this policy cannot clear preferences belonging to an installed extension. Open Privacy from the extension's own footer to use the reset control.

Data collection and sharing

Revolware does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data through this extension. The extension has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking cookies or telemetry. It does not read browsing history or other websites. It requests no optional or host permissions.

Network use and external links

The PDF engine, interface and fonts used by the extension are bundled or provided by your system. PDF operations do not require network access. Extension pages block outbound application connections through their content security policy.

If you choose the support email link, your browser opens your email application. The extension does not attach your PDFs or file names. If you send a message, Revolware receives the email address, message and any attachments you choose to include, and uses them to respond to your request. Your email provider and mail delivery services process that message under their own policies. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request; you can ask us to delete it. Do not email confidential documents.

Chrome Web Store, browser updates and browser-managed downloads operate under Google's and your browser's own policies.

Scope and changes

This policy covers LocalPDF Suite version 1.3.0. Future changes to data practices will be described in an updated policy before they apply.
